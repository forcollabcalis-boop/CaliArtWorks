Who I am

I'm a Minecraft Artist with over 3 years of experience creating custom assets for developers, clients, and personal projects. I specialize in 3D modeling and help bring ideas to life when developers need custom content or don't have the time to create it themselves. What I do

3D Models – Mobs, weapons, items, furniture, entities, and other custom assets. Minecraft Skins – 16x16, 32x32, and soon 64x64 skins. Animations – Mob animations (idle, walking, attacking, death), item animations, and texture animations. 2D Textures – Custom textures for items, blocks, and other Minecraft assets.

My approach

I focus on creating assets that are both visually appealing and practical for in-game use. Every project starts with understanding the client's vision and requirements, followed by designing and refining the asset to match the desired style. I prioritize quality, optimization, and attention to detail to ensure that models, textures, skins, and animations work smoothly while maintaining a professional look. My goal is to turn ideas into polished Minecraft content that enhances the overall gameplay experience.
